# Policies & Bonus Techs

**Policies** occupy one of **5 policy slots** (expandable to 9 via Hereditary Rule/Democracy/Socialism/
Technocracy, then +1 more via Omnicracy). **Bonuses** are passive one-time modifiers with no slot.
Percentage effects stack **additively** (SCALING §2). All magnitudes **`[proposed]`**; eras follow
PROGRESSION.md (each item's era = the era of its row there). Every policy's advancement name == the
policy name.

## Watch-points

1. **Legitimacy snowball.** The Theocracy line (**+25 / +25 / +50 legit per era**: Theocracy /
   Propaganda / Deepfaked Reality) + Organized Religion & Schism (gold = legit) + Prediction Markets
   (end-of-combat gold ∝ surviving attack) + the legit-leverage buildings (Elysium, Monastery, Hagia
   Sophia ×2) form a strong loop. Watch the ceiling once combat is tuned.
2. **Democracy is a policy-slot unlocker** in v2 (E8), NOT the old legit-loss doubler — so **Freedom of
   Religion** (E6) is the sole legit-loss doubler.
3. **Damage stacking is additive but deep:** category doctrine (+50%) + Steel-line (+15…60%) + command
   auras (+50/100%) + Caste (+25%) + Adaptive (+5%/turn) + Eugenics (+2/era) + Liminite (+40%) can pile
   very high on one unit. Intended, but worth a look once combat is tuned.
4. **Removed from prior draft** (not in PROGRESSION.md): Golden Age, Naturalism, Manifest Destiny,
   "Digital Afterlife" (replaced by **Cosmic Myth**, E18), "Immortality" (replaced by **Biological
   Immortality**, E25). **Transhumanism** is retained as a real bonus (E12, tech "Psychology").
