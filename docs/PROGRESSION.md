# Progression Tree — Advancement → Unlocks

Units/buildings/specialists/wonders unlock via a *tech* whose name differs from the item; policies & bonuses keep their own name. **Warrior** is default. Thresholds: reducer techs show their discount; base thresholds grow `T(N)=T(N-1)+X·1.25^E·n·R` (progress X=5.6, food X=13.3, production X=7.4). `[proposed]`.

Legend: 🗡 unit · 🏛 building · 👤 specialist · 📜 policy · ⭐ wonder · ✦ bonus.
