# Wonders

Each occupies the dedicated **Wonder slot**, unlocks a placeable **incomplete** building, and needs
**N = 3 additional production-builds** to finish (nothing until complete; one wonder in flight at a time;
destroyed → restart). Monumentality/Megastructure Engineering cut N; Pilgrimage/Tourism/Star Hopping
scale finished yields. Multi-tile footprints per SCALING §8. Each wonder lists the **tech** that unlocks
it (from PROGRESSION.md). All **`[proposed]`**.

## Watch-points (⚠️ above)

1. **Legitimacy loop, again.** **Stonehenge +25/era** and especially **Hagia Sophia** (one-time
   legit-doubling + ongoing *production = 2× legitimacy*) plug straight into the Theocracy/Organized
   Religion/Elysium/Monastery loop. Hagia Sophia is the single biggest amplifier — a high-legit build
   now directly funds production every era, and doubling banks whatever you've stockpiled. This is the
   #1 thing to balance once the whole tree is in.
2. **Free-value spikes.** **Sistine Chapel** hands a free advancement pick every era with no threshold
   penalty — compounding tech acceleration that's easy to under-price. **Manhattan Project** front-loads
   2000 damage plus a persistent fallout-tile chip every battle — strong tempo against a fresh host and
   it never wears off.
3. **Power spikes.** Happy Valley **+8 levels** (+200%) on Mars, **Great Mirror +2 levels** across ALL
   terrestrial units & buildings (a global, not regional, upgrade — watch it stacking with Machu Picchu
   / Colonialism / Empire of the Stars), and Machu Picchu +2 on mountains are big multipliers;
   Skynet **+150%** on Terminators stacks additively on top of doctrines + Steel-line + auras (the deep
   damage stack flagged in policies). Ecumenopolis **+5000** economy on a planet tile is a late economy
   bomb — though gated to a rare terrain and era 25.
4. **Multi-tile commitments** (Great Wall 4×1, Hadron Collider 3×3 ring, Death Star 2×2) are large
   board investments — confirm the footprint model in SCALING §8 covers the 3×3 *ring* (hollow center)
   specifically.
5. **Pop axis.** Hanging Gardens (+1/gain) × Immortality (×2) × Tleilaxu/Hydroponics compounds the
   population-count engine that drives the whole economy — a potential runaway worth watching.
